## 1. Scope and evidence boundary

This audit rechecked P5-R1 through P5-R5, the R8 semantic field register, the R24 pricing-reader inventory, the accounting event/report matrix, the implementation tracker, roadmap, and operations guide. It then traced the principal mounted financial-report and KPI routes, their service inputs, and portal consumers. An exported controller or service without a mounted route is not treated as an active production path.

No database, MySQL endpoint, production system, external payment provider, NCM service, bank, or payment statement was accessed. No migration, seed, historical rewrite, or persisted-data inspection was performed. Route and transaction statements below are verified from current source, not evidence that a deployed instance or record population is current. No external cash receipt, complete source population, or persisted GL/subledger reconciliation is claimed.

The inventory covers known financial and financial-looking reports/KPIs surfaced by the repository's mounted routes and first-party portals. It is not proof that every external API client, historical export, dynamically constructed request, or deployed consumer has been discovered.

### Classification vocabulary

| Classification | Use in this audit |
|---|---|
| `POSTED_LEDGER` | Derived from posted/reversed journal entries; does not certify complete source events or subledger reconciliation. |
| `SUBLEDGER` | Uses accounting documents, allocations, Treasury records, or party-specific source records; may still be partial. |
| `OPERATIONAL` | Uses order, fulfillment, payment-flag, delivery, or other workflow records rather than posted accounting as its authority. |
| `ESTIMATE` | Combines estimated, current, legacy, or incomplete cost/tax inputs; not a verified accounting result. |
| `UNRECONCILED` | Its selected internal records or subledger population has not been established as complete and reconciled to control accounts/external evidence. |

Mixed views are assigned the least authoritative useful classification, and their component bases are called out separately.

## 3. Distributor `totalRevenue` investigation

The Distributor Direct Orders page computes:

```js
directOrders
  .filter((order) => order.payment)
  .reduce((sum, order) => sum + (order.amount || 0), 0)
```

The actual mounted `GET /api/distributor/orders/direct` route returns the authenticated distributor's `DIRECT_DISTRIBUTOR` orders, ordered newest first, through `getPagination`. The Manufacturer portal requests `page` and `limit=10`. It sends no date filter. The KPI is therefore a paid-flagged **loaded-page order total**, not a period total or distributor-wide total; it also remains unchanged by the page's search and order-type filters.

It includes only rows whose operational Boolean is true and sums their accepted order amount. It does not test a customer invoice balance, cash transaction, receipt allocation, journal, settlement/remittance, refund, or reversal. It does not require COD, direct customer collection, or company remittance, so it cannot distinguish those populations. It omits unpaid rows even if a linked invoice has been partially or fully collected; it can retain a true flag after a later return/reversal because it does not join those events. Under the self-delivery workflow, a verified customer collection can set `Order.payment` before the company receives a distributor remittance. The direct-order creation and status paths themselves reject paid declarations, so no reliable posted-sales population can be inferred from the current KPI.

A company-wide posted ledger summary exists, but no distributor/direct-order-scoped posted-sales endpoint with a verified matching source population was established. Replacing the KPI with an aggregate GL amount would change its scope and could introduce unrelated sales. Accordingly, the amount calculation is preserved and the label now reads **“Paid-Order Total on Loaded Page”**, with a notice that it is not posted sales or company Treasury receipts. The three count cards are also identified as loaded-page counts.

## 5. Source, date, and amount caveats

- `Order.payment` is not a receipt ledger and does not encode partial-payment state. Generic invoice receipts and reversals change documents/allocations/Treasury/journal records without synchronizing this Boolean.
- A delivered order is not necessarily collected; a distributor collection is not company remittance; a Treasury record is not independent bank/cash evidence. NCM collection, distributor remittance, and generic customer receipts remain separate source events.
- `Order.amount`, some operational dashboards, and item JSON contain Float/Number compatibility values. Decimal use inside a query or helper cannot recover precision already lost in a legacy source value.
- Periods are not uniform: journal reports use configured BS report dates; product analytics uses Kathmandu day boundaries; legacy Finance and manufacturer reports use their own configured windows; customer location sales are all-time; direct-order KPI has no date filter; distributor statement dates are applied to several different event timestamps; the Tax Report uses runtime-local month boundaries.
- Returns and reversals have separate statuses and dates. Some operational product reports include completed return amounts by return date, while the Distributor Direct Orders KPI and customer location summary do not subtract returns/refunds. Receipt reversals do not necessarily alter order flags.
- The user-entered evidence references and internal cash events described in reports are not independent bank statements, provider evidence, or physical cash counts.

## 6. Proposed future regression tests

These are recommendations, not new behavior claims. No calculation or API logic changed in this increment, so no test was added to assert UI copy.

1. **Distributor direct-order KPI:** ten-row page boundaries, paid and unpaid rows, rows outside the current page, search/type filters, no date filter, and an invoice fully settled while `Order.payment` remains false. Verify the KPI remains explicitly page-scoped and never implies Treasury cash.
2. **Payment/collection separation:** verified distributor customer collection before remittance, pending collection declaration, COD delivery without collection, remittance after collection, generic partial/full customer receipt, and full receipt reversal. Verify order Boolean, invoice balance, cash/Treasury, and posted report remain distinct.
3. **Product analytics:** exclude cancelled orders; include paid and unpaid orders equally; include only completed returns by `returnDate`; test order/return period boundaries in Kathmandu; and show that order-level discounts, delivery charges, receipt reversals, and posted journal status do not silently enter the product metric.
4. **Customer location analytics:** all-time accepted order total despite selected new-customer date filters; paid/unpaid inclusion; cancelled exclusion; completed returns/refunds excluded from the current contribution formula; missing/legacy cost inputs; and explicit Number/string serialization.
5. **Admin Finance Dashboard:** ensure posted-ledger values are separately derived from included posted/reversed journals while operational COGS/expense/asset inputs remain marked estimates. Exercise returns, unpaid/paid direct orders, partial/full receipt, reversal, COD delivery without collection, and date boundaries.
6. **Tax Report and legacy statements:** verify posted VAT versus operational tax projection labels, current/status filters, runtime timezone behavior, return/expense populations, Decimal serialization/rounding, and failure/missing accounting event notices.
7. **Manufacturer/distributor subledger views:** verify source-linked documents, legacy populations, request status, reversals, distributor collection versus remittance, and the precise date field/cutoff for each report.

Existing focused tests validate selected mocked accounting reports, financial statement periods, and product analytics calculations; they do not prove persisted accounting correctness. New report-source or UI-contract tests should be added when calculation or response behavior is changed, not to merely restate a static label.
