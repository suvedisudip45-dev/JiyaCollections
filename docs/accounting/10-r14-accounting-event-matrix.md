# R14 — Accounting Event and Reporting Matrix

**Review date:** 2026-11-16

**Status:** **PARTIAL** — current implementation is mapped at source-code level; no persisted data was inspected.

**Purpose:** Separate code behavior from internal accounting rules and unresolved decisions. This is not a statutory accounting or tax opinion.

## 1. Status vocabulary

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CONFIRMED EXISTING POLICY** | A business rule is explicitly recorded as owner-confirmed in the current accounting documentation. This does not prove complete implementation. |
| **IMPLEMENTED** | A matching code path is present. It is not proof of correct persisted balances or policy approval. |
| **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** | Selected paths have accounting effects, but source completeness, settlement, reports, reversal, or reconciliation are incomplete. |
| **PROPOSED INTERNAL POLICY** | A design recommendation for reliable internal books; it is not approved merely because it appears here. |
| **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION** | The code uses a default or multiple behavior exists, but an authoritative internal rule is not established by reviewed sources. |
| **NOT IMPLEMENTED / NOT FOUND** | No active end-to-end path was found in the repository review. |
| **BLOCKED BY DATABASE VALIDATION** | Persistence, actual balances, historical conversion, migration, or reconciliation cannot be established because no verified isolated database is available. |

## 2. Event-to-accounting matrix

Debit/credit descriptions below are economic directions, not authorization to introduce an account code or change current postings. Use existing mapped accounts. Where the source does not identify a usable account or policy is unresolved, block implementation pending an internal decision.

| Event | Preconditions and source identity | Expected internal accounting effect | Current source behavior / status | Policy, evidence, and unresolved items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Order placed, not delivered | Valid order/line snapshot and checkout/order ID | No earned sales/COGS merely because an order exists. A pre-fulfillment receipt, if one is later supported, is a customer advance/liability until the recognition event. | Storefront currently creates COD unpaid orders; accounting recognition is on delivery via outbox. No provider authorization/capture or general advance-receipt model was found. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Delivery recognition is **CONFIRMED EXISTING POLICY** in `04-target-accounting-domain-model.md`. Customer advances and prepayment allocation are **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION / NOT IMPLEMENTED**. |
| Delivered sale | Delivery confirmation, original `Order` and delivery/fulfillment identity, stable sale event | Debit actual receivable/tender for gross amount; credit product revenue and output tax according to the effective snapshot; record delivery revenue/tax separately; recognize COGS and the corresponding inventory or manufacturer obligation according to ownership. | `postDeliveredOrderAccounting` posts delivered-sale effects and accounting outbox retries. R15 removed delivery-status writes to `Order.payment`; delivered COD remains a receivable, not company cash. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Customer revenue/tax at delivery is **CONFIRMED EXISTING POLICY**. Exact tender by actual receipt, including COD vs gateway, is incomplete. |
| Inventory-owned sale issue | Actual fulfilled quantity and carrying cost layer | Debit COGS; credit inventory control for the same cost and issued quantity. | Distributor fulfillment consumes canonical inventory and carries cost-layer references. Manufacturer fulfillment and some legacy stock paths do not consistently issue canonical inventory. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Valuation method must be approved and used consistently; current layer FIFO does not cover all inventory. |
| Manufacturer-consigned sale COGS | Delivered order, accepted approved unit COGS snapshot, manufacturer and order identity | Debit COGS; credit manufacturer payable for the agreed eligible cost. Do not book consigned stock as company inventory before sale/delivery. | The target rule is documented; production and selected delivered-sale paths create cost/payable effects. Legacy manufacturer costs, order reservations and payable summaries coexist. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Manufacturer consignment and approved cost basis at delivery are **CONFIRMED EXISTING POLICY** in existing design docs; complete source population is not reconciled. |
| Discount / promotion | Approved discount snapshot tied to order and line allocation | Reduce the selling consideration/revenue base once; allocate consistently to lines for return/credit calculations. | Checkout and Admin order flows apply discounts; return/exchange snapshots retain value data. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Allocation across split shipments and later returns needs scenario-level acceptance evidence; do not infer tax treatment from UI totals. |
| COD collection by distributor | Delivered self-delivery order, assigned distributor, authenticated actor, full expected amount, payment method and customer OTP when enabled | Record actual full collection against the source order; do not infer collection from delivery. Collection is separate from remittance and does not by itself mean company bank cash. | `DistributorCashEvent` records the full amount under a stable order key. OTP-verified events are `VERIFIED`; without OTP a distributor declaration is `PENDING_VERIFICATION` and `Order.payment` remains false. R16 uses shared Decimal amount formatting, rejects missing actor identity, and excludes unverified amounts from remittance capacity. No partial collection, collector-custody journal, correction or reversal is available. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | OTP is the current operational evidence, not independent cash-count evidence. Custody account mapping, non-OTP verification process and partial/failure policy are **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**. |
| NCM COD remittance | Delivery/settlement batch and explicitly entered actual net deposit | Recognize gross COD receivable at delivery. On recorded remittance, debit selected Treasury for the actual deposit and clear gross receivable through supported fees/variance; delivery status is not collection or remittance evidence. | R15 requires `actualNetDeposit` as a decimal string and uses the existing batch transaction for cash/journal/allocation. Gross COD in non-reversed batches is reported separately from delivery status. Per-delivery actual collection and independent bank matching remain absent. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | An Admin-entered amount is not independently bank-verified. Gross COD/carrier-fee treatment is **CONFIRMED EXISTING POLICY**; exact variance/late cutoff and full 1170/2180/2185 reconciliation remain open. |
| Distributor COD remittance | Verified source-linked distributor collection events, open COD receivable, transfer evidence and idempotency key | Debit selected receiving Treasury and clear eligible distributor receivable once; never post revenue a second time. Partial remittance leaves only verified unremitted funds available. | The `submitDistributorRemittance` helper limits submissions by open receivable and verified, unremitted `CUSTOMER_COLLECTION` events; pending collection declarations do not count. No production caller or routed submission endpoint was found. The routed `COMPANY_COLLECTS` settlement-request flow is separate and does not use this cap. **SERVICE-LEVEL ONLY / PARTIAL.** | Remittance evidence is Admin-reviewed but external statement matching is not established. Whether the routed settlement-request flow must be source-linked is **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**; collection-event reversals and legacy data validation remain **BLOCKED / NOT IMPLEMENTED**. |
| Direct-order/customer receipt | Original order/sale, actual amount, method, actor, evidence/reference and stable event identity | Debit the approved cash/bank/clearing location and credit the eligible receivable, or separately classify an approved advance/unallocated receipt. | Admin, manufacturer, and distributor direct-order flows still accept paid flags without a common source-linked actual-receipt record. The order cash-receipt helper is not routed from `orderRoute.js`; provider payment webhooks were not found. **NOT IMPLEMENTED / NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION.** | Approve direct-sale evidence, tender, cash-location/account mapping, advances, unallocated receipts, partial payment, correction and reversal rules before wiring. |
| Generic customer collection | Open invoice/document, party, amount and idempotency key | Debit mapped Treasury/clearing; credit the allocated receivable. Discount/fine or excess must be separately classified and supported. | Treasury collection and `financialController` adjustment paths differ; they do not form a canonical all-order tender ledger. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Confirm adjustment accounts/recognition, overpayment handling and whether to consolidate endpoints. |
| Cancellation before handoff | Order and inventory reservation identity; payment and order-linked financial-activity checks | Release reservation and cancel operational source only if no payment/financial effect exists. If payment occurred, require a separate evidenced refund/credit and linked reversal as applicable; cancellation itself is not a refund. | R15 blocks customer/Admin cancellation when `Order.payment` or order-linked journal, document, cash transaction, or distributor cash event exists. It does not create a refund or reversal. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | No canonical multiple-payment/refund model; partial refunds, pending/completed refund caps and linked reversal remain **NOT IMPLEMENTED / NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**. |
| Full/partial customer return | Original sale/line, eligible sold quantity net of prior returns, approved RMA, physical receipt and inspection | Reverse eligible consideration/tax and the related COGS only for the returned quantity/value; restore only accepted restockable stock. Record damaged/missing disposition separately. Create customer refund payable/credit or refund cash on its own evidenced event. | Return request, review, inspection, stock, return journal and payable paths exist; some inventory paths remain legacy. Platform refund and distributor-funded refund are distinct. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | The original delivered sale and its discount/tax snapshots must determine cap. Fee and original delivery-charge treatment are **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**. |
| Refund payment | Approved refund obligation and method; original return/exchange | Debit refund payable/customer credit and credit the actual mapped cash/settlement account when paid; retain original-sale and return references. | Platform refund payable is settled through payout; distributor refund uses evidence/review route. Reconciliation covers only selected populations. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Do not infer settlement from refund approval or RMA completion flag. General reversal/correction must preserve journal history. |
| Exchange / replacement | Original sale and return leg; replacement lines/stock; request key; price/tax snapshots | Treat as linked return plus replacement consideration and COGS/inventory changes; price difference is collected once or held as customer credit/refund. Do not create a duplicate independent sale for the original value. | Exchange service stores price difference and route-specific settlement; poster handles revenue/tax/inventory effects. No universal in-progress cancellation or complete reversal. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Rules for late failure, no-delivery, lower-value credit, delivery charges, and repeated exchange need explicit decision/test cases. |
| Supplier/manufacturer purchase obligation | Approved purchase or production source, party and evidence | Debit supported inventory/expense/asset/landed cost and eligible recoverable tax; credit vendor/manufacturer payable. | Production cost layers and payable/journal path exist. Inbound-purchase journal helper has no active purchase receipt/cost-layer caller identified. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Supplier invoice evidence and inventory ownership determine classification. Unlinked purchase poster is not a complete procurement workflow. |
| Supplier payment / advance | Approved bill/document or explicit advance request, payment evidence and party | Bill settlement debits AP and credits actual Treasury. An advance debits an advance asset and credits Treasury until allocated/refunded; never expense it merely because cash left. | Payout confirms bill allocation. AP finalizer rejects incomplete accounting-party bill allocation; no general AP advance lifecycle identified. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | FIFO allocation exists in selected subledger paths; treatment of excess, advance, or unallocated payment is **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**. |
| Supplier return / credit note | Original receipt/bill, return quantities and physical evidence | Reduce AP or record cash refund; reduce inventory/expense and reverse only eligible tax/cost. No general application reversal found. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Supplier return costing and bill eligibility rules need testable internal policy; no historical amounts were inspected. |
| Distributor sale/remittance/netting | Distributor sale, source document, cash event/remittance evidence and set-off reference | Record separate distributor AR/AP documents; clear only documented/approved set-off and actual cash. | New distributor settlement can net approved documents and post cash/journal atomically; legacy earnings/request pathways coexist. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED.** | Contractual authority for set-off and handling of legacy logistics obligations remain **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**. |

## 3. Report and reconciliation map

Reconciliation is a comparison of complete populations at the same cutoff, not a dashboard comparison. A calculator can be source-scoped and still be incomplete.

| Source population | Subledger / evidence population | General-ledger comparison | Report/cutoff and present limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order lines delivered by tax point | Sale snapshot, delivery state, payment/settlement, return/exchange links | Delivery sale, tax, delivery, COGS and payable/tender journal lines | Posted statements use journal transaction dates; analytics may use `Order.date`. Sales source-to-journal population not proven persisted. |
| Customer/distributor receipts | OTP-verified distributor collection events, other source receipt records, remittance batches, allocations, and bank/cash/gateway statements | Treasury and AR control journal lines | Only distributor self-delivery full-COD collection has a current source event; direct-order receipts and a unified all-tender ledger are absent. R16 cash-held figures are service calculations, not proof of custody or complete reconciliation. |
| Customer and distributor returns/refunds | RMA, item inspection, approved amount, refund evidence and AP/AR allocation | Return/tax/COGS and refund settlement journals | Product analytics uses request/return date and item refund snapshots; distributor, gateway and legacy populations are not unified. |
| Exchange requests and replacements | Original sale, returned and replacement lines, inspection, difference collection/credit | Exchange sales/tax/inventory/COGS and AP/AR settlement lines | No demonstrated complete export or end-to-end reversal; cutoffs can differ across legs. |
| Supplier/vendor bills and payments | Party documents, source bills, credit notes, allocations and payment evidence | Full 2110 and related inventory/expense/tax accounts | Management AP reader combines legacy and selected document paths; no full supplier control reconciliation. |
| Manufacturer obligations and settlements | Production/COGS bills, local logistics, legacy order costs, credits and payout allocation | Full manufacturer payable control (including 2160 where mapped) | Summary combines document, journal-line and settlement-request populations; legacy unattributed lines remain. |
| Distributor obligations/receivables | COD/order documents, cash events, remittance batches, approved offsets and settlements | Full distributor AR/AP controls (including 1190 and mapped payable accounts) | New source-specific calculators are partial; legacy request/logistics paths coexist. |
| Inventory by SKU/location/owner/condition | Canonical quantity ledger plus cost layer, returns, in-transit/damaged/lost and legacy stock schedule | Inventory control account (1140 for platform-owned inventory) and COGS | Dashboard includes legacy cost fallback; valuation reconciliation excludes uncosted/mismatched units; legacy writers may bypass ledger. |
| Expenses/procurement | Expense source, invoice/VAT review, paid/AP split, purchase receipt | Expense, input tax, AP and inventory accounts | Expense has two writers; purchase poster has no discovered receiving/cost-layer caller. |
| CPA and delivery cost | Verified redemption snapshots, CPA bills/payouts; NCM batch/fee and bank receipt | 2170, 6430, 1170, 2180, 2185 and related mapped lines | CPA/NCM reconciliations are source-scoped; legacy settlement and historical records remain. |
| Cash/bank/wallet/gateway | Treasury register plus independent cash count/bank/provider statements and transaction matching | Mapped cash/bank/clearing lines | Treasury-to-GL is not bank/provider reconciliation. 1180 mixes wallet and gateway clearing; no provider statement importer found. |
| Loans, assets and capital | Lender amortization/statement; fixed-asset register and physical verification; owner/share register and evidence | 2510, fixed asset/accumulated depreciation, equity and drawings lines | Source-scoped reports omit external evidence/opening completeness; many source fields remain Float. |

## 4. Decision register

The following are not resolved by choosing whichever behavior the code currently uses:

- Customer advances, pre-delivery collection, unapplied receipts, surplus payouts and supplier advances.
- Payment tender taxonomy, partial/mixed tender, payment failure and gateway fee/chargeback treatment.
- Customer refund of original delivery charge, return fee exceptions, distributor/company refund funding and late-return period.
- Exchange cancellation, no-delivery, lower-value customer credit, fee and price-difference cutoffs.
- Purchase receiving/landed cost, supplier VAT evidence and cost-layer allocation.
- FIFO across all inventory versus the currently partial production-layer FIFO; treatment of damaged, lost, in-transit and uncosted stock.
- Expense account/category mapping and capitalization boundary; depreciation policy; loan interest/accrual policy.
- Owner contributions versus share issuance or loan, drawings identity, and whether profit distributions ever become a payable.
- Cash-equivalent classification of 1180 and account mapping for ambiguous categories.

Every decision must be labeled **PROPOSED INTERNAL POLICY** until the business confirms it. Tax classification and registration facts remain separate from this internal event design.
