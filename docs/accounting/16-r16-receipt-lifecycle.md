# P4.2-R16 — Source-Linked COD and Direct-Order Receipt Lifecycle

**Review date:** 2026-11-16  
**Status:** **PARTIAL** — distributor self-delivery COD and the routed company-collection settlement cap were hardened. Direct-order payment declarations are now rejected unless supported by a receipt workflow, but that source-linked receipt workflow, partial collection, and persisted validation remain incomplete.

## 1. Scope and evidence boundary

This specification traces the current order, collection, remittance, journal, and report entry points and records the limited R16 implementation. It does not claim that every receipt is persisted, externally verified, or reconciled to the general ledger. No database or provider was accessed.

No schema or migration was added. The existing `DistributorCashEvent`, `DistributorRemittanceBatch`, `DeliveryFinancialSettlement`, `NcmSettlementBatch`, order, cash transaction, accounting-document, and journal models remain separate source populations. A general receipt model is not introduced while tender, collection-custody, and cash-account rules are unresolved.

## 3. Supported distributor COD lifecycle

The following is the lifecycle currently supported by source code. It is not a universal payment state machine.

| State / transition | Actor and evidence | Amount and source checks | Accounting effect / next state |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EXPECTED` | Order/settlement source; no collection actor | Expected COD is the order amount plus delivery fee under the current self-delivery contract. | No receipt is created. Delivery may progress without asserting payment. |
| `COLLECTION_CHALLENGE_PENDING` | Authenticated distributor requests customer OTP; challenge binds order, distributor, and full expected amount | OTP payload has expiry, attempt limit, stable challenge identity, and amount-change detection. | No cash, receivable, or journal mutation. |
| `COLLECTION_VERIFIED` | Distributor submits OTP; customer-provided OTP is the existing full-collection confirmation | R16 calculates and compares amounts using shared Decimal utilities and NPR HALF_EVEN quantization. One order-keyed `CUSTOMER_COLLECTION` event is recorded. | The `DistributorCashEvent` is the recorded collection evidence. `Order.payment` may be true only for this verified state. No custody-account journal is posted. |
| `COLLECTION_DECLARED_PENDING` | Distributor confirms collection while SMS OTP is disabled | Full expected amount and payment method are recorded with authenticated actor. | Event remains `PENDING_VERIFICATION`; order remains unpaid for reporting purposes. It is excluded from verified remittance capacity. No review route for this event was found. |
| `PARTIAL`, `FAILED`, `CORRECTED`, `REVERSED` | No complete supported transition | No partial collection amount, failure event, reversal link, or correction event is recorded for this COD path. | **NOT IMPLEMENTED / NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION.** Do not infer these from delivery status or overwrite the original event. |
| `REMITTANCE_PENDING_REVIEW` | `submitDistributorRemittance` helper accepts transfer reference, HTTPS evidence, actor, and idempotency key; no production submission route/caller was found | The helper caps amount by open COD receivable and verified collections less pending/verified remittances. This is not enforced by the separate routed `COMPANY_COLLECTS` settlement-request flow. | A pending batch reserves amount; it is not business cash and does not clear the receivable. |
| `REMITTANCE_VERIFIED` | Authorized Admin selects an eligible NPR account and reviews evidence | Existing service validates the batch and allocated receivable within its transaction. | Cash transaction, journal, receivable allocation, review attribution and event link are created. Revenue is not posted again. |
| `INTERNALLY_RECONCILED` | Requires matching complete source, subledger, cash, and journal populations | No R16 process performs a complete reconciliation. External statement evidence is not connected. | **BLOCKED BY DATABASE VALIDATION / NOT TESTED.** |

The collection event is not a dedicated collector-cash asset subledger. `cashHeldByDistributor` is a Decimal report calculation over verified collection events less pending/verified remittances, not proof of physical cash custody. The cash-in-custody account mapping and recognition timing are **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION**.

## 4. Direct-order receipt contract and current gap

For Admin, manufacturer, and distributor direct orders, the current payment Boolean and selected `paymentMethod` do not satisfy the source-linked receipt contract. A safe future receipt event must include the order/sale source, actual amount, method, effective timestamp, authenticated actor, evidence/reference, stable idempotency identity, and allocation or unallocated classification. It must not be created by delivery completion or a paid toggle.

The codebase does not currently have one receipt model/API that can atomically represent the differing direct-order routes, a business cash location, and journal/cash transaction links. The current bounded R16 follow-up fails closed on true payment declarations rather than inventing a receipt or account mapping. No account codes or receipt policy are introduced. Until the evidence, actor, account, advance, unallocated-receipt, and direct-cash-sale rules are approved, direct-order receipt posting remains **NEEDS BUSINESS DECISION / NOT IMPLEMENTED**.

## 6. R16 implementation and decimal/report contract

- `distributorDeliveryOtpService.formatCollectionAmount` now parses the Float-backed order amount and delivery fee as decimal text, adds and quantizes with `accountingDecimal.js` / `quantizeNprAmount`, and serializes the OTP amount as a two-place string. The amount is exact over the stored source representation; this cannot recover precision already lost in Float storage.
- `verifyDistributorDeliveryOtp` compares normalized collection amounts using Decimal, not JavaScript Number.
- `distributorSelfDeliveryService.updateSelfDeliveryStatus` refuses to record a COD collection without an authenticated actor. The SMS-disabled declaration remains pending and does not mark the order paid.
- `orderController.buildOrderListItem` no longer derives payment from `DELIVERED` + COD. The response reflects the persisted payment flag only.
- `submitDistributorRemittance` requires an actor and limits a new batch to both open receivable and verified, unremitted `CUSTOMER_COLLECTION` events matched to open COD document source IDs. This helper still has no production caller or routed submission endpoint. The actual Admin-created `COMPANY_COLLECTS` settlement-request flow now applies the same source-matched collection cap when the request is created and again when it posts, and rejects unsupported receivable source types. Admin review requires an explicit actual-received amount equal to the request before posting; the authenticated Admin actor is retained. The transfer evidence/reference remains a claim and is not independently reconciled against a bank statement. `getDistributorCashPosition(s)` exposes decimal-string `verifiedCustomerCollections`, `remittedOrPending`, `cashHeldByDistributor`, and `availableToRemit`; the service-level cap remains authoritative.
- Existing NCM summary fields remain distinct: `codExpected` is expected settlement COD; `codOpenExpected` is expected COD in open settlement rows; `codSettledGross` is the gross COD included in non-reversed remittance batches; `codReceived` is a legacy alias for the sum of `DeliveryFinancialSettlement.codCollected`; `codToReceive` is the legacy expected-minus-that-field calculation. `codReceived`/`codToReceive` are not actual collection evidence and should be deprecated after an approved API migration.

Remaining relevant monetary conversions include Float-backed `Order.amount`, `DeliveryFinancialSettlement` COD/fee fields, Number arithmetic in Admin/manufacturer/distributor direct-order amount creation, Number-based NCM `financialController` reporting, and `NcmCourierAdapter.trackDelivery` conversion of `cod_collected` to Number. Direct-order receipt flags no longer cause monetary postings, but the order amount writers still need a separately scoped Decimal migration. The adapter field has no trusted persistence consumer found in this trace. These are **NOT IMPLEMENTED / REMAINING P4.2 WORK**; R16 does not perform historical conversion.

## 7. Idempotency, correction, and transaction findings

- Distributor self-delivery collection uses the stable key `delivery-collection:<orderId>` and a unique `DistributorCashEvent.idempotencyKey`; it represents at most one full collection per order. A changed-amount or partial retry is not supported as a second event.
- OTP challenge issuance, expiry, attempt limiting, and verification are order/distributor scoped. The collection event is created in the delivery status transaction with the status/audit effects.
- The unmounted `submitDistributorRemittance` helper uses a caller idempotency key, rejects changed amount/reference/evidence on replay, and creates the batch plus pending cash event together under serializable isolation. These properties have service-level tests only; no routed production submission was found.
- The routed settlement-request evidence flow passes `req.auth.accountId` (or `userId`) as `distributorAccountId`, and the service records `evidenceSubmittedByAccountId`. The Admin review controller passes an explicit `actualReceivedAmount`; the service rejects absent, invalid, partial, or mismatched values, requires the authenticated Admin account, and posts through `postDistributorBalanceSettlement` only within source-matched verified collection capacity. This is a conservative safety gate, not a complete cash-custody or bank-statement reconciliation policy.
- Existing Admin remittance review links the reviewed batch, cash transaction, journal, allocation, and event in one application transaction.
- No general collection correction/reversal transition was found. The transaction behavior above is code-level evidence only; database serialization, uniqueness/concurrency, rollback, and persisted source-to-journal correctness are **BLOCKED BY DATABASE VALIDATION**.

## 10. P4.2-R18 mounted customer-invoice receipt follow-up

The mounted `POST /api/finance/collect-receivable` endpoint is now distinguished from the separate legacy `financialController.js` collection export. Customer invoice IDs are processed by `customerReceiptService.js`; only active-customer NPR receivable invoices/debit notes that are open or partially paid may be selected. A confirmed receipt requires a caller idempotency key, authenticated receiver account, actual amount, active NPR Treasury account mapped to an active GL account, tender method, and evidence reference. Legacy receivables without eligible customer invoices are not edited through this path. NCM settlement confirmation remains an approval-request flow and is not recorded as an already-confirmed receipt here.

The service uses shared `accountingDecimal.js` helpers and NPR two-decimal HALF_EVEN rounding. The actual cash amount remains distinct from the settled invoice amount when an allowed discount/fine applies. It records a receipt `CashTransaction`, posts the customer-payment journal with the receipt as source, and allocates the settlement to explicitly selected invoices through `AccountingAllocation.cashTransactionId`. Cash receipt, source allocation, Treasury balance, and journal posting are issued in the same Serializable transaction. The Admin invoice form requires tender/evidence; invoice-less rows are visibly blocked. The API returns decimal strings.

Same-key replay requires matching normalized payload hash and reconciles the stored settlement amount, linked allocations, and expected POSTED journal; changed-payload reuse is rejected. The route and actor guard, receipt service, allocation helper, poster, and existing settlement-correction tests were exercised with mocks/in-memory fixtures. These tests do **not** prove database transaction rollback, concurrent duplicate handling, persisted source-to-journal integrity, or bank evidence.

**P4.2-R19 full receipt-reversal update:** Customer invoice receipts are corrected by receipt ID through the existing `SETTLEMENT_CORRECTION` maker-checker route; the request maker cannot approve their own correction. The full-only service requires reviewer identity, reason, and evidence, validates receipt metadata, posted journal lines, allocations, invoice balances, and Treasury linkage, then reverses actual cash and invoice settlement separately. It uses the canonical journal reverser, restores each invoice, creates immutable reversal allocations linked to their original allocations, and records the settlement-reversion and audit entries in the same transaction. The original receipt and allocations remain unchanged. The Admin history exposes one correction action per receipt across its invoices and displays reversal evidence. Partial reversals are rejected.

The additive `20261116171600_customer_receipt_reversal` migration adds a unique self-reference from a reversal allocation to its original allocation; it remains **unapplied**. An identical collection-key replay after reversal now reports `REVERSED` without reposting, and changed-payload reuse still conflicts. Focused mocked/in-memory receipt, correction, approval, subledger, and posting tests passed **121/121**, but do not prove persisted atomicity, rollback, concurrency, or source-to-journal integrity. No database, `localhost/clothing`, provider, or historical data was accessed. Persisted validation and external bank-evidence reconciliation remain **BLOCKED**; Float-backed invoice/source precision remains.
